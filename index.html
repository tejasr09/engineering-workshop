Quick facts for four common tools.

UseMeasure length, depth, diameter

UseMake round holes

Safety rules

RuleWhat to do
Wear protectionSafety goggles, apron and closed shoes at all times.
Tie backSecure long hair and loose sleeves near machines.
Right toolUse each tool only for its intended job.
Clear benchKeep your work area tidy and free of oil spills.
ReportTell the instructor about any injury or damaged tool.
No runningWalk, stay calm and never play with tools.
